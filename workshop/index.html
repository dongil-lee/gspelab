GSPE Lab Workshop

The GSPE Lab Workshop brings together scholars of international political economy (IPE), American politics (AP), comparative politics (CP), and political methodology to build academic networks across subfields. Each workshop features presentations and discussions on a broad range of topics in political economy. The workshop is held in person annually at the KDI School of Public Policy and Management in Sejong, Korea.
