Visiting Scholars

Mads Andreas Elkjær

He is currently visiting the KDI School (2026).

Mads Andreas Elkjær is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Political Science at the University of Copenhagen.

Elkjær’s research focuses on the political and democratic consequences of economic inequality in advanced democracies, with broader interests in political representation, comparative politics, and political economy.

Elkjær received a Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Southern Denmark.
