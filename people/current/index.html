Current Members

Faculty

ByungKoo Kim

ByungKoo Kim is an Assistant Professor of Data Science at the KDI School of Public Policy and Management.

Kim’s research lies at the intersection of international political economy and quantitative methods, covering global supply chains, the politics of trade governance, and the development of Bayesian models for networked data — including text-and-citation models for legal corpora and dynamic latent-space models for trade networks.

Kim received a Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Michigan.

Seungjun Kim (Lab Leader)

Seungjun Kim is an Associate Professor at the KDI School of Public Policy and Management.

Kim’s research examines the intersection of international political economy and foreign direct investment, with particular emphasis on government-business relationships, industrial policies, and geo-economic statecraft, and on how these dynamics affect the liberal international order amid intensifying geo-economic competition.

Kim received a Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Maryland, College Park.

Personal website

Dongil Lee

Dongil Lee is an Associate Professor at the KDI School of Public Policy and Management.

Lee studies the political economy of development, focusing on how formal institutions and informal incentives shape political behavior, representation, and state capacity in sub-Saharan Africa.

Lee received a Ph.D. in Political Science from New York University.
