Research Seminar Series

Each year, the KDI School runs a Research Seminar Series for each of its programs: Public Policy, Development Policy, Public Management, and Data Science. Although the GSPE Lab does not officially organize these seminars, lab members, as KDI School faculty, have hosted the following scholars in the Public Management program’s series. These seminars bring cutting-edge research on international and domestic political economy to the KDI School community.
